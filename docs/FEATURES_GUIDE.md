# 📘 HireReady Feature Guide & Evaluator Walkthrough

This guide provides a comprehensive walkthrough of the **7 core pillars** of HireReady.

---

## 1. 🧮 Aptitude Practice Tests
### What it is:
A full testing engine designed to simulate online assessments like **TCS NQT**, **Infosys HackWithInfy**, **AMCAT**, and **eLitmus**.

### Key Capabilities:
- **Full-Length Timed Mocks**: Live countdown timer with automatic warning states when under 5 minutes.
- **Sectional Coverage**: Quantitative Aptitude, Logical Reasoning, Verbal Ability, and Technical Computer Science Core.
- **Question Palette**: Live visual tracking of questions (Answered, Marked for Review, Unvisited).
- **Instant Result & Scorecard**:
  - Score / Max Score with pass/fail threshold.
  - Overall accuracy rate.
  - Section-by-section breakdown percentages.
  - Step-by-step mathematical & logical explanations for every question.
- **Topic Question Bank**: Filter individual practice questions by difficulty, topic, or search term.

---

## 2. 💻 Algorithmic Coding Sandbox
### What it is:
An in-browser competitive programming and interview coding environment modeled after LeetCode and HackerRank.

### Key Capabilities:
- **Top Campus Placement Problems**: Two Sum, Valid Parentheses, Best Time to Buy and Sell Stock, Maximum Subarray (Kadane's Algorithm), and Container With Most Water.
- **Multi-Language Stubs**: Ready-to-code starter functions in **JavaScript**, **Python**, **C++**, and **Java**.
- **In-Browser Sandboxed Runner**: Sub-100ms testcase verification against both visible and hidden edge cases.
- **Detailed Runtime Diagnostics**: Measures runtime (ms), memory footprint (MB), and visual diffs of expected vs actual outputs.
- **Editorial & Solution Tab**: Detailed Big-O space and time complexity explanations alongside optimal reference implementations.

---

## 3. 🏢 Company-Wise Preparation Tracks
### What it is:
Dedicated recruitment intelligence blueprints for tier-1 product giants and national mass recruiters.

### Supported Companies:
- **Google** (Super Dream, 32 - 55 LPA)
- **Amazon** (Super Dream, 28 - 45 LPA)
- **Microsoft** (Super Dream, 26 - 44 LPA)
- **Goldman Sachs** (Super Dream, 24 - 38 LPA)
- **TCS** (Prime, Digital & Ninja, 3.6 - 9.0 LPA)
- **Infosys** (Specialist Programmer & DSE, 3.6 - 9.5 LPA)

### Blueprint Contents:
- **Round-by-Round Timeline**: Online Assessment, Technical DSA, Low-Level Design / Systems Core, and HR/Leadership rounds with duration and actionable insider tips.
- **Syllabus Topic Weightages**: Visual progress bars showing relative topic importance.
- **Eligibility Criteria**: Minimum CGPA, eligible departments, and backlog policy.
- **Past Interview Questions & Alumni Advice**: Verified experiences from students placed in the 2025-2026 drive.

---

## 4. 🎙️ Mock Interview Scheduling & Live Room
### What it is:
An interactive mock interview suite connecting candidates with AI bar-raisers or peer evaluators.

### Key Capabilities:
- **Flexible Scheduling**: Choose target company, round focus (DSA, System Design, HR), and mode (AI Simulated, Peer, Mentor).
- **Interactive Live Session Room (`/interviews/session/:id`)**:
  - Simulated active interviewer feed with live speech waveform animation.
  - Candidate camera and microphone toggles.
  - Live shared code and pseudocode scratchpad.
  - Active prompt delivery tailored to the company round.
- **10-Point Evaluation Rubric**:
  - Technical Knowledge (1-10)
  - Problem Solving & Logic (1-10)
  - Communication Skills & STAR Framework (1-10)
  - Confidence & Body Language (1-10)
  - Strengths and actionable improvement notes.
  - Automatically feeds into the student's Placement Readiness Score!

---

## 5. 🎯 Placement Readiness Engine & Progress Tracking
### What it is:
A dynamic scoring model that calculates student readiness for campus placements (0 - 100%).

### Key Capabilities:
- **Circular Readiness Gauge**: Prominently displayed on the dashboard with benchmark tier ("Super Dream Candidate", "Dream Ready", "Tier-1 Ready").
- **Multi-Factor Algorithm**: Combines coding test cases passed, aptitude accuracy rates, streak consistency, and mock interview rubric scores.
- **Recruitment Skill Mastery Breakdown**: Visual skill meters across DSA, Quantitative Math, Logic, Verbal, CS Fundamentals, and Communication.
- **Weak Areas & Action Plan**: Proactively recommends specific topics to practice based on test errors.
- **Streak Tracker**: Gamified consecutive practice day counter with flame animation.

---

## 6. 📄 ATS Resume Builder Studio
### What it is:
A clean, interactive resume editor formatted to maximize ATS parse rates during automated campus shortlisting.

### Key Capabilities:
- **Live Real-Time Side-by-Side Preview**: Instantly renders changes as you type across contact details, education, experience, projects, and technical skills.
- **Real-Time ATS Compatibility Score**: Live meter evaluating section completeness, bullet formatting, and keyword density.
- **AI Bullet Point Enhancer**:
  - Paste any basic draft bullet (e.g. *"built an in-memory cache using redis"*).
  - Click **Enhance** to generate 3 quantifiable, action-verb-driven statements with metrics.
  - 1-click clipboard copy into your project or experience section.
- **Print & PDF Export**: Clean `@media print` CSS stylesheet strips away all platform chrome for clean, professional single-page A4 printing.

---

## 7. 📊 Placement Statistics & Analytics Dashboard
### What it is:
An institutional placement intelligence dashboard providing transparency into campus recruitment outcomes.

### Key Capabilities:
- **Core KPIs**: Highest Package (54.5 LPA), Average Package (15.2 LPA), Median Package (12.8 LPA), Total Offers (1,245), and Placement Rate (94.2%).
- **Salary Distribution Visualizer**: Interactive visual breakdown across 3.5-6 LPA, 6-10 LPA, 10-20 LPA, and 20+ LPA bands.
- **Department-Wise Performance Table**: Compare CSE, IT, ECE, EEE, and Mechanical placement rates and CTCs.
- **Top Recruiter Directory**: Offers count and hiring tiers for major visiting tech corporations.
- **Recruiter Skill Demands**: Tracks YoY demand for DSA, Cloud, Microservices, and Full-Stack frameworks.
